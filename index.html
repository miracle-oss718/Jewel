About Praiselisa

Crafted with care. Made for you.

Praiselisa Goldsmith and Ring Store is a jewellery workshop and ring store specialising in creating, repairing, customising and resizing jewellery.

Whether you have a design in mind, a ring that no longer fits, or a favourite piece that needs repair, our goal is to give your jewellery the care and craftsmanship it deserves.

From personalised rings and custom jewellery to careful repairs and resizing, we work with you to create pieces that fit beautifully and last.
